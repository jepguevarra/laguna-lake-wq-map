    About the data

    The values come from the LLDA 2009 to 2012 Annual Water Quality Report on the Laguna de Bay and its Tributary Rivers. Each figure is an annual average of monthly samples. For coliform, it is the annual geometric mean.

    Lake stations (9) measure the open water. River stations (34) sit on the tributaries that drain the surrounding towns into the lake. Where a river has an upstream (U) or midstream (M) station, the plain number is the downstream station near the river mouth.

    Pass/fail is judged against the DENR Administrative Order No. 34 Class C criteria that the report uses. Class C covers fisheries, recreation and industrial supply. Several parameters have no Class C limit.

    Limitations

    • Station positions are approximate (about ±1.5 km). The report includes only a picture map, which was georeferenced against known river mouths.
    • The “Lake shoreline” overlay is the OpenStreetMap boundary of Laguna de Bay, thinned to about 10 m. It shows where the lake really is; the station positions drawn on top of it still carry their ±1.5 km.
    • Several stations started only in 2011 or 2012, so earlier years are blank.
    • Some 2009 coliform values in the report are implausibly high (up to 10¹⁵). They are excluded from the map and charts.
    • The town listed for a river is where the station is. Rivers also carry pollution from towns upstream.
